Before you start

Veil is for editing photographs of consenting adults. By continuing you confirm that every person in the photos you edit is an adult and has agreed to be photographed and to have the photos edited.

Veil never swaps in a face taken from another picture. Your photos stay on this computer.

Pair this phone

  1. On your computer, open Veil and click Phone.
  2. Turn on Allow phone access, then click Show QR code.
  3. Point this phone's camera at the QR code and tap the link that appears.

This phone also needs the Tailscale app, signed in to the same account as your computer and switched on.

      Use Veil from your phone

      Your phone connects through Tailscale, a private network between your own devices. Nothing is opened to the public internet.

      1. On your phone, make sure Tailscale is on. Then open the camera and point it at this code.
      2. Tap the link that appears. The phone will say it is waiting.
      3. Come back here and click Approve next to the phone below.

      Phones

        GPU worker

        A worker is another computer of yours with a strong graphics card (at home, or a rented one). Veil sends it only the heavy jobs: selecting, finding the person, filling in, faces and generative fill. It reaches it over Tailscale only, the worker keeps nothing, and if it cannot be reached Veil does the job here and tells you.

        1. On the worker, run veil-worker pair. It prints a code that works once, for 10 minutes.
        2. Type the worker's Tailscale address below (shown when the worker starts), and the code.

        Watermarks

        A watermark is added only when you export. Make as many as you like (for example one for social media and one for proofs). On your phone you can switch one on or off and pick between these.

          Language model

          The model only reads your typed request and suggests one edit. It cannot change your photos by itself. Online models get your words only, never the photo, unless you confirm it for that one request.
